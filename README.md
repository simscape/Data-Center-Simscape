## Electrical Analysis
<table>
  <tr>
    <td class="text-column" width=1200>
        On the electrical side, you design a double-conversion UPS validated 
        against IEC 62040-3 and ITIC, evaluate a Solid State Transformer 
        with CHB-DAB power delivery to GPU racks, and assess MW-scale 
        network stability under firmware faults, weak grids, and UPS 
        degradation.
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>

<table>
  <tr>

        the chiller, cooling tower, and pump temperature controller, and
